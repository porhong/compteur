# Privacy policy for My Compteur

_Last updated: 21 October 2026_

My Compteur is a system monitor that runs entirely on your PC.

**What it collects:** nothing. The app has no accounts, no analytics, no telemetry and no ads, and it never connects to the internet.

**What it reads on your PC:** performance counters (CPU, memory, GPU, disk and network use), the names and resource use of running apps (to show the busiest one), and, when you use Clean up PC, the sizes of temporary, cache and Recycle Bin files. This information is shown on screen and never leaves your PC.

**What it stores:** one small settings file with your display choices (position, shown metrics, clean-up options). Uninstalling the app removes it.

**What Clean up PC deletes:** only the categories you have selected in the app, as described there. It never touches your documents, downloads, browser history, cookies or passwords.
